\enonce 
Exprimez en coordonnées polaires :
$\displaystyle y^2 \frac{\partial^n f}{\partial x^2} - 2xy
\frac{\partial^n f}{\partial x  \partial y} + x^2 
\frac{\partial^n f}{\partial y^2} - x {\partial f\over
\partial x} - y \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}.$
\finenonce
